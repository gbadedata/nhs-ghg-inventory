# NHS trust estate GHG inventory, 2025/26

Generated by `nhs-ghg run`. Activity data: NHS England ERIC 2025/26 site data (1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026). Factors: DESNZ 2025 conversion factors. Both under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Method and every assumption: [METHODOLOGY.md](../METHODOLOGY.md).

**Scope:** 205 NHS trusts, 2,165 of 2,923 sites reporting energy. Estate energy, water and waste only: not the full NHS footprint (no medicines, anaesthetic gases, travel or supply chain).

## Reconciliation

- **Energy total:** 11.21 billion kWh, against the 11.2 billion kWh NHS England publishes for 2025/26. Adding the separately reported CHP gas input (3.07 billion kWh) would give 14.28 billion, so the published total confirms that 'Gas consumed' already includes CHP feed (D2).
- **Waste:** 205,247 t quantified; 162,743 t of clinical, offensive and on-site-treated waste is reported but not converted, because DESNZ has no factor for those routes (D9). This is the largest known gap in Scope 3.
